(the new-doctor line; the 2am googling). Post 3 should go further: no lesson,
no "you can too", no closing list. The model is the 2am paragraph: one honest,

## Rules for this one

- Open on a scene, not a statement. A place, a day, a body detail.
- Every section has at least one sentence nobody else could have written
  (like the numb legs, the head static, the 2am search).
- No advice to the reader. No "you" until the very end, if at all.
- No headings that sound like advice ("Give yourself permission…").
- Don't resolve it. The last line should leave the question open.
- Content warning and photo credit, same as posts 1 and 2.
